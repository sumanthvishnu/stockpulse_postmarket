# Personal photoreal Telegram bot

Jork-style flow: **send a photo → tap a preset → get a batch**.

- Model: [Z-Image-Turbo](https://huggingface.co/Tongyi-MAI/Z-Image-Turbo) (photoreal, fast)
- Runs on a rented RTX 3090 (Vast.ai / RunPod), not on your laptop
- Private to your Telegram account
- Adults only (blocks underage *prompts*; no “looks 18” visual filter)

**If you are the owner: read [YOUR_STEPS.md](YOUR_STEPS.md).** That is the whole checklist.

This folder is independent of StockPulse. Copy it somewhere private if you do not want it in that repo.
