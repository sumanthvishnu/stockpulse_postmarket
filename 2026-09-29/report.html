Executive Summary

The Indian markets witnessed a broad-based decline today with the Nifty 50 closing at 22,716.20, down 64.05 points (-0.28%). The broader indices such as Nifty Next 50 and Nifty Midcap 150 also fell significantly, with declines of 1.21% and 0.99% respectively. Market breadth was negative with 1,605 declines against 1,040 advances, indicating a narrow rally. The India VIX decreased by 1.65% to 13.41, remaining near the top of its 21-session range. Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers in the cash market, offloading Rs 9,980.22 Cr, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) bought Rs 6,952.71 Cr. No sanity flags were breached today.
